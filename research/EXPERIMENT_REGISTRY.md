## E1 — Null model at full scale

**Purpose.** Test claim 1 (kernel ratio is at chance) and estimate R with uncertainty for
claim 2, at 100 replicates instead of the advisor's 10.

**Input.** 65 unfiltered graphs: the 23 paper models + WordNet (`main__*`), E3 (5), E4 (18),
E5 (18) (decision D004). Graphs are rebuilt from the stored definitions with
`rs_dic_llm.graph_build` and saved as edge lists with the source sha256.

**Method.** `experiments/e1_full_null.py` imports `summarize` and `compute_kernel` unchanged
from `experiments/null_model.py` and rewires each graph with `nx.directed_edge_swap`
(5 x edges swaps, at most 300 x edges attempts, the settings of `null_model.py`). Per replicate:
kernel_ratio, circulation_rate, cycle counts by length 2-7, swap-failure flag. Per graph:
null mean and SD (population SD), z, R = obs / max(null mean 2-cycles, 0.5), bootstrap 95 % CI
on R (10,000 resamples of the replicates), R with failed replicates removed, R_analytic for
comparison. Seeds are `sha256("e1|graph|replicate")`, so a rerun gives identical output.

**Command** (repo root; resumable, safe to re-run after an interruption):
```bash
python -m experiments.e1_full_null --out results_2026-10-01_Local/e1_null_unfiltered --reps 100 --workers 10
```

**Outputs** in `results_2026-10-01_Local/e1_null_unfiltered/`: `summary.csv`,
`null_model_all.json`, `results/<graph>.json`, `replicates/<graph>.jsonl` (raw replicates),
`graphs/` (snapshots + `manifest.json`), `run_info.json` (git HEAD, versions), `e1.log`.
